1# Open your Steam Library.

3# Under the General tab, find the Launch Options text box.
4# Paste the following command:

python3 /usr/local/bin/play_startup_song.py "/usr/local/share/midi/slim_shady.mid" & %command%

5# copy/save the file to its correct location: /usr/local/bin/play_startup_song.py
